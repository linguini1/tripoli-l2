\subsection{Nosecone}

The nosecone is a Von Karmaan nosecone shape. The length of the nose is 7
inches, and the length of the shoulder is 2 inches. The shoulder thickness is
$\frac{1}{4}$ inch.

The height of the nosecone was selected to be within the ~225mm print-height
limit for most consumer printers.

\subsubsection{Manufacture}

The nosecone of the rocket will be manufactured out of PETG for simplicity. This
allows the (relatively complex) shape to be made on a consumer 3D printer, which
is generally accessible. It does not require an "advanced" 3D printer which has
the capability to print in filaments like ABS. PETG also has the property of
being radio transparent, which is required for a nose-cone GPS tracker.

The nosecone should be printed using gyroidal infill for high strength. It is
also recommended to use a minimum of 4 vertical walls in the slicer settings for
durability.

\subsection{Assembly}

The nosecone is affixed to the forward body tube using 4 radial M3 screws. The
nosecone is fitted with 4 heat-set inserts evenly space along the shoulder. This
allows the nosecone to be removed, but also tightly affixed for flight.
